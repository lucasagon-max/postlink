# Create links with preview

Learn how to build, automate, and scale your social image workflows with
SocialCards.


Links with preview are the links shared on social media and displayed as a card with an image. Here is an example:

![Example of a Facebook post with a link with preview](https://social.cards/assets/upload/2024/12/link-with-preview-on-facebook.jpg)

To create such a link, first you need to create the image using our editor. Then, **Save** the image.

After you save the image, a **Link** option will be enabled in the tool bar. Click it to create your link.

![Screenshot of the SocialCards editor with an arrow pointing to the Link button in the topbar section](https://social.cards/assets/upload/2024/12/open-link-modal.jpg)

> In order to create links with preview, you need to have an active PRO subscription.

Complete the form with your details.

![Screenshot of the SocialCards form to create links with preview](https://social.cards/assets/upload/2024/12/create-link-form.jpg)

After you create the link, you will get a link like `https://example-com.href.cards/123abc` which you can start sharing it on social media platforms. When the users will click it, they will be redirected to your link.

You can find your links by accessing the **My links** section. Here you can manage you links and check the stats for the last 7 days.

![Screenshot of SocialCards menu expanded to highlight the My links entry](https://social.cards/assets/upload/2024/12/my-links-section.jpg)
